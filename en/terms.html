4. Rules for getting along

Game Fever only works if everyone treats each other fairly. In particular, the following is not allowed:

  • harassing, insulting, threatening or discriminating against others – in the chat, in contributions or at meetings,
  • illegal content, e.g. incitement to hatred, depictions of violence, pornography or content harmful to minors,
  • content you have no rights to, e.g. other people’s photos or texts. Photos of game boxes and game rules may also be protected by copyright,
  • personal details about others without their consent (e.g. addresses or phone numbers), including in game descriptions,
  • advertising, spam, mass requests and links to third-party offers,
  • false information in the game catalogue, e.g. deliberately wrong barcodes or games that do not exist,
  • attempts to disrupt the app, circumvent security measures or retrieve other people’s data automatically.

6. Reporting content

If you notice content that violates these rules or the law, let us know:

  • In the app: long-press a chat message, review or recommendation and choose “Report”; games, public game nights and profiles via the “⋯” menu or “Report profile”. You can also block the person there.
  • Without an account or by email: to support@gamefeverapp.de. Please describe as precisely as possible which content it is (e.g. name of the game or game night, the person’s display name, the time), why you believe it is illegal or violates these rules, and give your name and email address. Please also confirm that your information is correct and complete to the best of your knowledge.

We confirm receipt of reports by email, review every report carefully and promptly, and tell you what we decided. For reports in the app, you are told there what we decided (for example content removed or no violation found) and how you can appeal. The reported person does not learn who reported them.

Please do not abuse reporting. If someone repeatedly sends manifestly unfounded reports, we may disregard their reports for a period of time.

7. Moderation and appeals

Automatic check: chat messages, reviews, recommendations, game descriptions, titles and descriptions of game nights as well as display name and alias are automatically compared with a list of gross insults, hate speech, sexual terms and threats when saved. If a contribution contains such a term, it is not saved and you can rephrase it. There are no other automated decisions.

Reported content is reviewed by the app’s administrators. Every decision is made by a human; there are no automatic suspensions based on reports. We take into account the circumstances, the seriousness of the violation and the rights of everyone involved. Depending on the case, we may:

  • remove individual content: replace a chat message, remove the text of a review (the stars remain), delete a recommendation or a photo, take a public game night out of “Discover” or restore an earlier version of a description,
  • warn you; from the second warning on as a “final warning”,
  • reset your display name, profile picture or alias,
  • suspend your account for 1, 7 or 30 days or permanently. A suspended account cannot use the app; you can still delete your account at any time.

We inform you about any action in the app, including the reason and, where useful, a short explanation of what we noticed. If your account is suspended, you see the reason and duration when you open the app.

Disagree? Write to support@gamefeverapp.de within six months and explain why you think the decision is wrong. We will then review it again and tell you the result. This also applies if you reported something and disagree with our decision. You may also go to court at any time.

If we have indications of a criminal offence that threatens the life or safety of people, we inform the competent authorities (Art. 18 of the Digital Services Act).
