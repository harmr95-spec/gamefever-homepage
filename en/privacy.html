2. The key points in brief

  • To use the app you need an account with an email address, a password and an alias of your choice. You do not need to give your real name.
  • What you create (collection, game nights, chats, results) is only visible to the people it is meant for – details in section 5.
  • The data is stored with our service provider Supabase on servers in Frankfurt am Main, Germany.
  • Game descriptions written by users are translated automatically by Microsoft in the EU – only the text, without any information about you (section 10).
  • No tracking, no advertising, no analytics services.
  • You only receive push notifications if you allow them on your phone (section 12).
  • You can delete your account yourself in the app at any time.

3. Scope

This privacy policy applies to:

  • the app “Game Fever: The Game Night Planner” (“Game Fever” for short) – currently in a testing phase and not yet published in an app store,
  • the website www.gamefeverapp.de, including the pages for confirming your email address and resetting your password.

4. Account and sign-in

The app can only be used with an account; it is intended for people aged 16 and over (see terms of use). We process:

  • Email address – for signing in, confirming your account and resetting your password. Other users cannot see your email address.
  • Password – stored only in encrypted form (as a hash); nobody, including us, can read it in plain text.
  • Alias and display name – under this name you are visible and can be found by others in the app. You may use a pseudonym.
  • Profile picture – optional, see section 6.
  • Agreement to the terms of use – which version you accepted and when. This lets us show which terms apply to your account and ask for your agreement when something changes.
  • Language – the language chosen in the app (or “like device”), so we can show you texts and game names in your language. The app sends the current language with every request to the server.

We send you emails to confirm your account and to reset your password. They are sent via the email server of STRATO GmbH (Berlin, Germany) as our processor; for this, your email address and the content of the email (e.g. the confirmation link) are transmitted to Strato (see section 9).

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (providing the app you want to use).

Protection against bots (Cloudflare Turnstile): When you register, sign in, use “Forgot password” or change your password, we use the Turnstile service of Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) to check that a human and not an automated program is using the form. To do this, the app or website loads a small check box from Cloudflare. Your IP address and technical characteristics of your device and browser (e.g. browser type, language setting, screen size and the result of short computing tasks in the browser) are sent to Cloudflare; Turnstile may briefly store data in the browser’s or app’s storage for this purpose. Cloudflare does not receive what you enter in the form (email address, password). The result of the check – a code that is valid only once – is sent by the app together with the sign-in to Supabase, which has it confirmed by Cloudflare. According to Cloudflare, the data is used only for this check and not for advertising. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in protecting accounts and the service against abuse by bots (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR); reading the device information is strictly necessary for the secure access you requested (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). The transfer to the USA is safeguarded by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which Cloudflare is certified (Art. 45 GDPR), and additionally by EU standard contractual clauses. More information: Turnstile privacy notice and Cloudflare privacy policy.

5. What you create in the app – and who sees it

The app is made for game nights with friends. What you create is stored so that you and your fellow players can see it on all devices. It is only visible to signed-in users of the app – and even then only to the following extent:

  • Profile (display name, alias, profile picture): to all signed-in users, so you can be found and invited.
  • Friendships, requests and blocks: only to the people involved.
  • Game collection and editions (which games you own): to you, your friends and people you attend a game night with.
  • Game nights: depending on your setting, only to invitees, to your friends or publicly. For public evenings, all signed-in users see the title, description, time, town and approximate distance. Only the host and people who have accepted see the exact address.
  • Chat messages: to the participants of the respective game night or competition. We delete them automatically 12 months after the game night or competition has ended.
  • Competitions, results and statistics: to the players and the participants of the associated game night.
  • Game reviews and recommendations for fellow players: to all signed-in users, together with your display name.
  • Contributions to the game catalogue (games created, names, editions with barcodes, photos, “correct / wrong” votes): public games are visible to all signed-in users, private ones only to you and your friends or fellow players.
  • Game descriptions (“What’s it about?” and “How to play”): to all signed-in users who can see the game. Anyone can add to or change them; we store every version with its author and time so that an earlier one can be restored. Only the administrators see the author. Please don’t include personal information. For the automatic translation see section 10.
  • Notifications (e.g. invitations, friend requests, reminders of game nights, notices from the administrators): only to you. We delete read notifications after 90 days and all others after one year at the latest.
  • Reports (e.g. “wrong details”, “inappropriate content” or a reported person with reason and explanation): only to the app’s administrators, who review them. Games, editions and profiles can be reported, and so can individual content by other users: chat messages, reviews, recommendations and public game nights. When such content is reported, we additionally store a copy of the reported content as it was at the time of the report (for game nights: title and description) and link the report to the profile of the person who wrote it – so the administrators can review the report even if the content has been changed in the meantime. Only the administrators can see the copy; it is deleted six months after the report has been closed. The person who reported learns what was decided (e.g. content removed or no violation found), but not which action was taken against whom; the reported person does not learn who reported them.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. Reports are additionally reviewed on the basis of our legitimate interest in a safe and accurate service (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Action in case of violations: If an individual piece of content is not respectful towards others, the administrators can remove it: a chat message is replaced by “Removed by the moderators”, the text of a review is removed (the stars remain), a recommendation is deleted, a public game night is taken out of “Discover” (the group keeps it), a photo of a game is deleted. The person who wrote the content is informed in the app, including the reason and, where useful, a short explanation. If someone does not treat others fairly (e.g. harassment, offensive profile, spam), the administrators can also warn the account, reset its display name, profile picture or alias, or suspend the account temporarily or permanently. There are no automatic suspensions based on reports; every action is decided by a human. For this we store the action with its reason, any short explanation, time, duration and the administrator who took it; the person concerned is informed in the app. A suspended account can no longer use the app and is no longer shown to others in searches; deleting the account remains possible at any time. We keep this information until the account is deleted. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in respectful and safe interaction (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

6. Profile picture, game photos and camera

You can optionally choose a profile picture or a photo for a game from your device’s photo library. The picture is resized and converted to WebP format on your device; only this new picture is uploaded. Additional information from the original photo (e.g. where it was taken) is not transferred.

Profile pictures and game photos are stored with Supabase and can be retrieved via a non-guessable web address so they can be displayed quickly in the app.

The app only uses the camera when you scan another person’s QR code to become friends, or the barcode of a game box. No pictures are stored or transferred – only the recognised number: for a barcode, it is sent to our server to find the game. If we don’t know the barcode, our server asks GameUPC, a freely accessible directory that matches barcodes to board games; only the number is transferred. If you assign a barcode to an edition, we store it with the edition together with the note that it came from you (so others can confirm it), and report the match to GameUPC – there only with a random-looking identifier that cannot be traced back to you. The app only accesses your photos when you choose a picture yourself, and only uses the camera after your permission.

7. Location and radius search

The app does not ask for your location and does not track it. To search for game nights near you, you enter a country and a town yourself. Approximate coordinates (rounded to about 1 km) are determined from this. Your search location is only stored on your device.

When you create a game night, the approximate coordinates of the town and postcode are determined and stored with the evening so others can find it in the radius search. The street is not used for this.

To determine the coordinates, the location you entered is sent:

  • on an iPhone to Apple’s location service;
  • on Android devices, in the web version and if Apple’s location service does not respond to the Nominatim service of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (St John’s Innovation Centre, Cambridge, United Kingdom). Town, postcode and country and, for technical reasons, your IP address are transmitted. The European Commission has issued an adequacy decision for the United Kingdom.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, as the radius search is a feature you actively use.

8. Game data from BoardGameGeek

The game catalogue contains details and pictures from BoardGameGeek (BoardGameGeek, LLC, USA). Our server retrieves the game data; no data about you is sent to BoardGameGeek. If you explicitly search BoardGameGeek for a game in the app, our server only forwards your search term – without any information about you.

Pictures from BoardGameGeek, however, are loaded directly from BoardGameGeek’s servers when you view such a game. For technical reasons, BoardGameGeek receives your IP address and information about your device or browser. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in displaying game pictures in accordance with BoardGameGeek’s terms of use (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). BoardGameGeek’s privacy policy applies.

If you open a link to another website in the app (e.g. to BoardGameGeek or a stored game link), the privacy provisions of the respective provider apply there.

9. Servers and data processing (Supabase, Strato)

For accounts, database, file storage and live updates we use the service Supabase (Supabase, Inc., San Francisco, USA). We have a data processing agreement with Supabase in accordance with Art. 28 GDPR. The data is stored in the region eu-central-1 (Frankfurt am Main, Germany). For live updates (e.g. a new chat message appears for everyone at once), Supabase keeps a copy of the transmitted message for a few days for technical reasons and then deletes it automatically – even if the message was deleted earlier.

When the app accesses the server, log data is generated for technical reasons (e.g. IP address, time, interface accessed). It serves secure operation and is only kept by Supabase for a short time (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Supabase may use sub-processors; you can find a current list at supabase.com/privacy. Where data is transferred to the USA, this is safeguarded by EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR) or – where the providers are certified – by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).

Email (Strato): We send the account emails (account confirmation, password reset) via the email server of STRATO GmbH, Otto-Ostrowski-Straße 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany. Your email address and the content of the email are transmitted to Strato. Our mailboxes for contact@gamefeverapp.de and support@gamefeverapp.de are also hosted by Strato (see section 18). Strato processes the data as our processor in its data centres in Germany; under the contract, processing generally takes place in the EU. We have a data processing agreement with Strato in accordance with Art. 28 GDPR. The legal basis for sending account emails is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR; for the mailboxes it is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in being able to answer requests), or (b) if it concerns your account.

10. Translation of game descriptions (Microsoft)

Game descriptions (“What’s it about?” and “How to play”) are written by users in one language. So that they can also be read in the app’s other languages, our server sends the text to the Azure AI Translator service of Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited (One Microsoft Place, South County Business Park, Leopardstown, Dublin 18, Ireland) after it has been saved, and stores the translation with us. In the app it is marked as “Automatically translated”; the original can be shown at any time.

  • What is transferred: only the text of the description and the languages. Your name, alias, account or any other information about you is not transferred; the request comes from our server, not from your device. Reviews, chat messages and other content are not translated.
  • Where: We use the service’s European address, so the translation is only processed in Microsoft data centres in the European Union.
  • No storage at Microsoft: According to Microsoft, texts sent for translation are only used while they are processed, are not stored and are not used for training (“No Trace”).
  • Agreement: Microsoft acts as a processor; the Microsoft Products and Services Data Protection Addendum applies (Art. 28 GDPR).

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in offering game descriptions to all users in their language (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). As you decide yourself what goes into the text when writing, we ask you not to include any personal information.

11. Storage on your device

The app only stores on your device what it needs to work:

  • your sign-in, so you do not have to enter your password every time you start it,
  • your search location for the radius search (see section 7),
  • your language choice,
  • cached pictures and data, so the app loads faster.

Signing out removes the sign-in. You can delete everything else by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your device settings.

12. Push notifications

If you allow it on your phone, the app sends you push notifications – for example about invitations and requests, who is coming or not, reminders of your game nights, friend requests and notices from the moderators. You choose which groups you receive in the app under Profile → “Push notifications”; notices from the moderators cannot be switched off. You can withdraw your permission at any time in your phone’s settings.

  • What we store: an identifier for delivery to your phone (push token), whether it is an iPhone or an Android device, the app’s language, date format and time zone (so the notification arrives in your language and with the correct time) and the groups you have switched off.
  • Delivery: Our server passes the text and the push token to Expo’s push service (650 Industries, Inc., Palo Alto, USA). Expo forwards the notification to Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging, Android) or Apple (Apple Push Notification Service, iPhone), who deliver it to your phone. The text says what it is about – e.g. the name of the person inviting you and the title of the game night – and, depending on your phone’s settings, can also be seen on the lock screen.
  • Deletion: We delete the push token when you sign out of the app, delete your account, uninstall the app (as soon as the push service reports this) or do not open the app for six months.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, § 25 (1) TDDDG), which you give via your phone’s prompt and can withdraw there at any time. Where data is transferred to the USA, this is safeguarded by EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR) or – where the providers are certified – by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). More information: Expo privacy policy.

13. Testing phase with Expo Go and Google Play

During the testing phase, the app runs inside the app “Expo Go” by Expo (650 Industries, Inc., Palo Alto, USA). Expo’s privacy policy applies to Expo Go itself (expo.dev/privacy).

Once the app is published on the Google Play Store, Google processes data such as your Google account and the time of the download when you download it. We have no influence on this; Google’s privacy policy applies (policies.google.com/privacy).

14. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself at any time: in the app (Profile → “Delete account”) or without the app at gamefeverapp.de/konto-loeschen, after signing in there with your email and password. If you no longer have access, write to us at the address in the legal notice; we will then delete the account within one month. In any case, the following happens immediately:

Deleted

  • email address, password, alias, display name and profile picture,
  • all connections to others: friendships, requests, blocks, invitations and recommendations others have written for you,
  • your game collection, including editions,
  • games you created yourself – unless others now have them in their collection or they have been played in a competition (then they stay in the catalogue without any reference to you),
  • your participation in future game nights and the future evenings you created,
  • your notifications as well as warnings, suspensions and other actions concerning your account,
  • reports by others about you or your content, including the copies of reported content,
  • the explanations in reports you sent yourself. Reports that are still open remain without the explanation until the administrators have reviewed them, because they concern another person; the copy of the reported content is deleted six months after the report has been closed, as always.

Replaced

The content of your chat messages, reviews and recommendations is completely replaced with “User deleted”, because it could contain personal information. Your star ratings continue to count towards a game’s average without text. For past game nights you created, the description and street are removed and the title then only reads “Game night”.

Kept anonymised

Competitions, results and participation in past game nights are kept so that your fellow players’ rankings and histories stay complete. The same applies to game descriptions you have written or changed: they are part of the shared game catalogue and are kept. There you only appear as “Deleted user”, without any reference to you (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Data contained in backups is overwritten as part of the regular backup cycle.

15. No sharing, no tracking, no advertising

We do not sell data and do not share it for advertising purposes. Data only goes to the services named in this policy, to the extent necessary for the respective feature.

The app and the website do not use any tracking, analytics or advertising services (e.g. no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics, no Facebook SDK) and no cookies for tracking purposes.

16. Data security

All connections between the app, the website and the servers are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). In the database, access rules for every table determine who may read and change which data; they apply independently of the app. In addition, we take technical and organisational measures in accordance with Art. 32 GDPR.

17. Website gamefeverapp.de

17.1 Hosting

The website is provided via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., San Francisco, USA); the domain is operated via STRATO GmbH (Berlin, Germany). When the site is accessed, GitHub automatically records server logs that may contain the following data:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • date and time of access
  • address accessed
  • browser type and operating system
  • referrer URL

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure and stable operation). According to GitHub, these logs are stored for up to 90 days. Where data is transferred to the USA, this is safeguarded by EU standard contractual clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More information: GitHub privacy statement.

17.2 No cookies, no external content

The website does not set cookies and does not use tracking. Fonts, scripts and images are loaded directly from gamefeverapp.de, not from third-party servers (e.g. no Google Fonts). The only exception is the Cloudflare Turnstile check box on pages with a sign-in (see section 4).

The website is available in German and English. Without your own choice, it follows your browser’s language. If you switch the language yourself, this choice is remembered in your browser’s local storage (not a cookie); it is not sent to us and can be deleted in your browser settings. The legal basis is § 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act), as storing it is necessary for the language selection you requested.

17.3 Confirming your email, resetting your password, deleting your account

The links in the account emails lead to pages on this website. There, the code from the link is sent to Supabase to confirm your email address or save your new password. On the “Delete account” page, your email and password are sent to Supabase to sign you in and delete the account; before that, the Cloudflare Turnstile security check runs (see section 4). The sign-in only applies to this process and is kept at most until the tab is closed in the browser’s session storage (not a cookie). The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

18. Contacting us by email

If you contact us by email, we process your email address and the content of your message to answer your request (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, or (b) if it concerns your account). Our mailbox is hosted by STRATO GmbH (Berlin, Germany) as our processor (see section 9). The data is deleted once the request has been dealt with and no statutory retention obligations apply.

19. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR)

Simply write to us at the email address given in section 1.

20. Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the federal state (Bundesland) where you live or where the controller is based.

21. Changes to this privacy policy

We adapt this privacy policy when the app or the legal requirements change – for example, when new features are added. You can always find the current version at www.gamefeverapp.de/en/privacy.html.
