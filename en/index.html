Game Fever is the app for game nights with friends: plan evenings, see who owns which game, run competitions with a live scoreboard – and know at the end who takes the title.

How it works When does it start?

The idea

Less organising, more playing.

A good game night starts long before the first roll of the dice: who has time? Who brings what? Which games do we even have? Today that is scattered across group chats, notes and memory.

Game Fever brings it all into one place. You plan the evening together, see at a glance which games the group has between you, and on the night itself you play a competition – with a schedule, points and a winners’ podium on your phone instead of on a beer mat.

How it all started: with our own game nights – several short games in a row, every game gives points, whoever is ahead at the end wins. That very game fever is still at the heart of the app – there it is called multi-game. Everything else a game night needs has grown around it.

In short

The key terms

So everyone is talking about the same thing – this is what things are called in the app.

📅

Game night

in the app: “Events”

A get-together with a date and place. Private, for friends or public – with replies, chat and the group’s games.

🏆

Competition

umbrella term

Anything where you play against each other and a winner is decided. Can belong to a game night or start spontaneously.

🏅

Multi-game

several games, one winner

Several games in a row. Every win brings points – if you like, later games count more.

📚

Collection & edition

who has what

Your games. If needed with the exact edition (publisher, year), so it is clear which version ends up on the table.

Features

From the first “Who has time?” to the winners’ podium.

1 · Plan

Plan game nights without group chat chaos

Create an evening, invite friends and instantly see who is coming. Everything else is discussed right there with the evening.

  • Invite by alias or QR code, replies at a glance
  • Chat for plans, snacks and car pools
  • Calendar with your next dates and a countdown

2 · Who has what

The group’s games – from all collections

Everyone keeps their own game collection. For the evening, the app shows which games the group has between them and who can bring them. Editions and expansions can be specified exactly if you want.

  • “2 friends own it” – visible on every game page
  • Reviews and a short explanation for every game
  • Filter by player count, duration and type of game

3 · Community

Find players and show what you can do

You can find public evenings near you under “Discover” – for example an open games meetup in a café. Your profile shows wins, statistics and your strengths.

  • Radius search by city, without sharing your exact location
  • Join openly or by request to the hosts
  • Recommendations from fellow players after an evening together

Competitions

Three ways to win.

A competition is set up in a minute: choose the type, the players, the games. While you play, everyone sees the score live on their own phone.

🏅

Multi-game

several games, one winner

Several games in a row, e.g. Skyjo, Jenga and darts. Whoever wins collects points. With “rising points” the last game counts most – it stays exciting until the end.

🎲

Single game

one long game

One game, everyone plays – such as a board game that lasts all evening. The app records who won.

⚔️

Round robin

duels

A game for two, e.g. backgammon or chess. Everyone plays everyone once, the app creates the pairings.

Your rules: rising points or every game counts the same · stop as soon as the winner is certain, or play everything · in a tie a tiebreaker or the last win decides. Plus dice, timer, chess clock, score pad and starter picker right in the app.

Game catalogue

A catalogue everyone maintains together.

So nobody has to type in every game by hand, Game Fever builds a shared catalogue – from board game classics to party games without any materials.

🔎

Search instead of typing

Search for a game, tap it, done – with player count, duration, picture and a short explanation. Alternative and multilingual names are found too.

📷

Scan the barcode in progress

Hold the box up to the camera and the game lands in your collection – with the right edition.

🧩

Editions & expansions

Expansions belong to their base game, editions from different publishers stay one game. That way there are no duplicates.

🤝

Checked by the community

If a game is missing, anyone can add it. Wrong details can be reported with a tap and are reviewed.

Where the game data comes from: for board and card games, a connection to BoardGameGeek is planned (name, player count, duration, year of publication, picture). The details are taken over unchanged and shown with their source; the app loads pictures directly from BoardGameGeek. We write descriptions and classifications such as “luck” or “strategy” ourselves.

Our principles

Built for groups of friends, not for reach.

Data-minimal

An alias and an email address are enough. No real name, no tracking, no advertising SDKs.

You decide who sees what

Only friends and people you share an evening with see your collection and evenings. Only what you make public is public.

No ads

During the testing phase the app is free and ad-free.

Data in the EU

The data is stored on servers in Frankfurt. You can delete your account in the app at any time.
